# FASHION — Design System

## Product direction
Contemporary Egyptian fashion e-commerce with a fashion-editorial, independent-label voice. Designed for mobile-first browsing and considered shopping.

## Visual system
- **Style:** Warm editorial minimalism, asymmetric image-led layouts, generous whitespace, fine rules, restrained terracotta accents.
- **Palette:** Paper `#EAE5DC`; soft paper `#F5F2EC`; ink `#25241F`; muted ink `#77736C`; clay `#A65C43`; olive `#8C8A7D`; border `#D4CEC3`; charcoal `#25241F`.
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond for wordmark and expressive editorial headings; DM Sans for navigation, body, prices, and controls.
- **Shape & spacing:** Mostly square corners, 1px rules, spacious section rhythm; controls have clear 44px minimum touch targets.
- **Photography:** Distinct unsplash.com fashion photographs, warm natural light, varied portrait and full-body framing. Reserve image ratios to prevent layout shift; lazy-load images below the fold.
- **Motion:** Short, restrained fades and image crop transitions. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## UX priorities
1. Clear accessible names, keyboard operation, visible focus, descriptive image alternatives.
2. Shopping actions available without hover; selection and form feedback appear inline.
3. Responsive two-column mobile product grid, no horizontal overflow.
4. Semantic headings, explicit labels, live feedback for cart and newsletter.
5. Local persistence for cart and wishlist; checkout visibly marked as a demonstration.

## Avoid
Generic centered hero layouts, repeated identical promo cards, excessive rounded corners, decorative gradients, fake reviews, false urgency, inaccessible icon-only controls, and unsupported manufacturing claims.
